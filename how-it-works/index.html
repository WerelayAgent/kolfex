How it works

Kolfex runs pari-mutuel markets on how fomo and pump.fun traders perform. No bookmaker sets the odds: the pot does.

Read the full docs→

Five steps

  1. 1

    Pick a trader

    Each market lists fomo or pump.fun traders and runs for 1 to 3 days. Back the one you think makes the most PnL while the market runs, from the moment it opens to the moment it ends.

    fomopump
  2. 2

    Odds come from the pot

    Every bet goes into one pot. A trader's odds are their share of it, so they move with each bet until betting closes.

    55%45%
  3. 3

    Winners split everything

    When the market settles, everyone who backed the winner shares the whole pot in proportion to their stake. A 3% fee is taken on each bet.

    3% fee on each bet
  4. 4

    Settlement

    When the market ends, each trader's PnL is worked out from the snapshots taken at the open and every day since, and the winner is proposed. The proposal can be challenged for the dispute window before it becomes final. If the result can't be read, or nobody backed the winner, the market is voided and every bet is refunded in full, fee included.

    Dispute window, set on-chain
  5. 5

    Points for every bet

    Win or lose, every bet earns points, and your rank climbs with the SOL you play: Paper hands at 0.1 SOL, Legend at 10.

    See the ranks

Questions

Every rule in full, from how PnL is measured to when a market is voided, is in the docs.

Can I back more than one trader in the same market?+−

Yes. Each bet is its own stake. Only the bets on the winning trader are paid; the rest go into the pot they share.

What counts as PnL?+−

Profit on trades closed while the market runs, plus how much the positions still held gained or lost over the same time. Only the market's own run counts. The full formula is in the docs.

What if a trader's figures can't be read?+−

Then the result can't be worked out and the market is voided. Every bet comes back in full, fee included.

Can the odds change after I bet?+−

Yes, until betting closes. The multiple you see is today's; what you get is your share of the final pot. Try the maths to see how a bet moves it.

When can I collect my winnings?+−

Once the result is final: when the dispute window ends after it's proposed if nobody challenges it. Claim from your portfolio.
