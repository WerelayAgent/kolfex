Anyone can list a market from a template by locking a deposit in the token. The team lists its own markets with no deposit.

The flywheel

Two things move the loop. The platform spends most of what it earns on buying the token, and anyone who wants to list a market has to hold some. Neither depends on the token's price: the buyback runs on fees from betting, whatever the token trades at.

What it does

Deposit to list a market

Creating a market locks a deposit in the token. It comes back once the market settles with enough bets, and is burned if it doesn't. The rules are under creator deposits.

Bought back and burned

The platform's share of every fee, and half of the token's trading fees on pump.fun, buy the token on the market and burn it.

What it doesn't do

You don't need it to bet: bets are in SOL. It doesn't change odds or payouts.

A voided market refunds every bet in full, fee included, so nothing is split. The fee is fixed per market when it is listed; the team can change it for new markets, up to 5%.

Buyback and burn

  • The platform's share of every settled market goes to the fee splitter, a contract that keeps the buyback money and the team's money in separate accounts.
  • The splitter also receives the token's creator fees from pump.fun and splits them half and half.
  • Every 15 minutes, when there is something to spend, it buys the token through Uniswap (on its pump.fun bonding curve, before graduating).
  • Everything it buys goes to the dead address, 11111111111111111111111111111111 (Incinerator). The contract checks the tokens arrived there on every buyback, so the buyback money can't leave any other way.
  • Creator fees pump.fun pays in the token itself are burned the same way.

Creator deposits

Comes back
The market settles with at least its minimum volume of bets (0.5 SOL at launch), or the team voids it for a reason that isn't the creator's, or nobody resolves it in 7 days.
Burned
The market settles below its minimum volume, nobody backed the winner, or the team voids it as invalid (a made-up or malformed trader). The reason is written on-chain.
Amount
Shown on the create page before you confirm.

Creators earn 30% of their market's fee in SOL once it reaches its minimum volume, and claim it from their portfolio.
