## ⚡ Overview

**Kolfex** (`$KFX`) brings high-speed pari-mutuel prediction markets exclusively to **Solana** and the **pump.fun** ecosystem. Users back their favorite crypto Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) with **SOL**, competing across real-time performance metrics and leaderboard rankings.

- **Native Currency:** SOL
- **Ecosystem:** Solana & pump.fun
- **Ticker:** `$KFX`
- **Domain:** [kolfex.fun](https://kolfex.fun)

---

## 🎯 Game Modes

1. **Duel (Head to Head):** Back one of two competing KOLs. The trader with the highest PnL by market close wins the pot.
2. **Battle Royale:** Multi-creator showdown (up to 16 outcomes).
3. **The Line:** Predict whether a KOL crosses a target return benchmark before expiry.
4. **Clash:** Ecosystem tournaments pitting top pump.fun and community traders head-to-head.

---

## 🪙 $KFX Token & Flywheel

- **Buyback & Burn:** Market and trading fees are routed directly into automated buybacks on pump.fun / Raydium, sending tokens straight to the Solana burn address (`11111111111111111111111111111111`).
- **Creator Listing Deposits:** Listing a new custom market locks a deposit in `$KFX`, returning upon market settlement.
- **Deflationary Velocity:** As more pots open and trading volume escalates, `$KFX` supply permanently contracts.
