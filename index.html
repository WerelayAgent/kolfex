How a market plays out

Four steps from your pick to your payout. No order books, no bookie: one pot, split between the people who called it.

  1. frVSor
    1Pick a matchup

    Two traders head to head, up to 8 in a battle royale, or one against a line, over 1 to 3 days.

  2. 2.61 SOL
    2Back a trader

    Your SOL joins one pot. Every bet shifts the odds, and they lock when betting closes.

  3. fomo
    3The market ends

    Each trader's PnL since the start is worked out from the snapshots, and the winner is proposed.

  4. 2.61 SOL+0.07+0.31+1.04
    4Winners split the pot

    Once the result stands unchallenged for the dispute window, the pot minus a 3% fee pays out pro rata to everyone who backed the winner.

How a market settles

When the market ends, each trader's figure is worked out from the snapshots and the winner is proposed. Then the clock starts.

Snapshots, start to end

Every trader is read when the market starts and again as it runs. Each read is kept, so a result can be checked line by line.

Time to dispute

A proposed result can be challenged for the dispute window before anyone can finalize it.

Full refunds if it's voided

No result 7 days after the market ends, or nobody backed the winner: every bet comes back, fee included.

3% fee, held until it settles

The fee is locked when the market is created and only taken when it resolves.
